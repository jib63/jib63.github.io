About

Software Architect

I design and build complex software systems at Merck Group in Molsheim, with a focus on Life science — I develop software deployed in system participating to biopharma processes

A significant part of my work sits at the intersection of industrial IT and security: I'm specialized in IEC 62443 (industrial cybersecurity), the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), and the security requirements that govern connected medical and industrial systems.

I'm also deploying Model Based System Engineering (MBSE) solution in my architecture group with the Capella software.

I hold two patents in radiation dose mapping for angiography imaging systems, and I graduated from ISIMA Clermont-Ferrand (2006).
